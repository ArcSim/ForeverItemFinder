# Forever Item Finder changelog

## 1.3.7

### New Features
- **Compare with your gear**: hold Shift over an item in the finder to compare it with what you're wearing, or hover the new Compare button on an item's page. Settings, Items can switch it to Alt, Ctrl, always or off.
- **Auction prices**: the finder's item tooltips now show the prices your auction add-on has scanned.

### Improvements
- **Item data**: updated with Forever's latest data: 17 hidden items now have names, 117 more old items show what Forever changed (Upper Blackrock Spire, Scholomance, Stratholme and Dire Maul gear and the Chromatic set among them), 2 newly found items, and drop places for about 1,900 more items.

## 1.3.6

### Improvements
- **Item data**: updated with Forever's latest data: 70 hidden items now have names, 300 more old items show what Forever changed (Dire Maul, Stratholme, Scholomance and Blackrock Spire gear among them), 27 newly found items including mounts, companions and cosmetic sets, and new drop places found by players.
- **Mounts**: 67 mounts now show that Forever made them Bind on Pickup.
- **Quest rewards**: 66 items from new Forever quests now name the quest that gives them.

## 1.3.5

### Improvements
- **Item data**: updated with Forever's latest data: 66 hidden items now have names, 89 more old items show what Forever changed (Scholomance, Stratholme, Blackrock Depths and Sunken Temple gear among them), and new drop places found by players.

## 1.3.4

### New Features
- **Minimap nodes**: within 50 yards of a marked vein, herb or chest, the minimap pin hides its icon and keeps its border, so you can see with Find Minerals or Find Herbs whether the node is up. Change the distance or turn it off under Settings, Favorites on the Map, Nodes.

### Improvements
- **Item data**: updated with Forever's latest hotfixes: 23 more new items, and 122 more old items show what Forever changed (Dire Maul, Sunken Temple, Maraudon and Blackrock Depths gear).

## 1.3.3

## 1.3.0

### New Features
- **Quest rewards**: rewards in your quest log are recorded without hovering them, and quest rewards shared by other players now name their quest.
- **Item changes on tooltips**: hold Alt over an item to see what changed from Classic right on its tooltip; Settings can switch it to Ctrl, Shift, always or off.

### Improvements
- **Dungeon finds**: items found inside a dungeon now show the dungeon's entrance on the map, including finds shared by other players.
- **How to get**: items without drop info yet now say what the player who found them saw, like the mob that dropped it or the dungeon it came from.
- **Item data**: 19 hidden items now have names, 12 old items show what Forever changed, and new drop places found by players.

## 1.2.0

### New Features
- **Item tooltips**: hover an item to see whether it is new or changed in Forever, and how to get it (Settings > Items > Tooltips).
- **Alt+click**: Alt+click an item in your bags or a chat link to open it in the Item Database.
- **Guild and group sharing**: finds also reach your guild and group.

### Bug Fixes

## 1.1.0

### New Features
- **What changed**: a button in the item box shows what changed from Classic.
- **Map counts**: see how many items each zone and dungeon holds (Settings > Places on the Map).
- **Zone filter**: show one zone's items in the Item Database.
- **Found bar**: see how many new items have been found so far.
- **Quest items**: a new Quest category.

### Improvements
- **Truer changes**: changed items now match what actually differs from Classic.
- **Map tooltips**: hover a marked item on the map for its full tooltip.

## 1.0.4

### Bug Fixes
- **Map pins in every zone**: favorites and marked items now show on the World Map in zones you have explored, instead of being hidden under the map.

## 1.0.3

### Improvements
- **Question marks last**: items with no picture (shown as a question mark) now sit at the bottom of the Item Database, so the list opens on items you can recognize.

### Bug Fixes
- **Map marks stay**: an item you mark with Mark on Map stays on your maps after a reload or a new session, until you take the mark off.

## 1.0.2

### Improvements
- **Troublemakers can be shut out**: players who abuse the sharing channel can be banned, and everyone's addon then ignores their finds and requests.
- **Fewer wrong mutes**: players who reload a lot, or run a newer version of the addon, are no longer muted by others.

## 1.0.1

### New Features
- **New finds count**: the Live Feed tab shows how many finds from other players came in since you last looked, and the minimap button's tooltip says it too.

### Improvements
- **Smooth logins**: when many finds arrive at once after you log in, the open window takes them in with a light update every couple of seconds instead of redrawing for each one.
- **Lighter map pins**: favorites on the World Map, continent maps and minimap use far less CPU when zooming, moving and loading in after login.
- **Sharing stays up**: if someone takes over or locks the sharing channel, the addon moves everyone to a backup channel on its own.
- **Only real names**: creature, zone and finder names on shared finds come only from the game and the addon's own data, so nobody can put their own words on a find.
- **Lighter in raids**: when a whole raid sees the same new loot, a few players' addons share it instead of everyone's.

### Bug Fixes
- **Every find gets shared**: items you find while no one else with the addon is online now reach other players once they come online, instead of staying only in your copy.
