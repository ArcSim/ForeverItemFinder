# Forever Item Finder changelog

## 1.2.3

### Improvements
- **Item sharing**: finds are shared with more players on your realm.
- **Live Feed**: shows only real news: items whose drop place was just found, or that changed from what we knew.
- **Quest rewards**: rewards you hover in your quest log are saved as quest rewards.
- **Item data**: today's hotfixes, and new drop places found by players.

## 1.2.2

### Improvements
- **Drop info**: items whose drop locations aren't known yet now name the creatures that drop them.

### Bug Fixes
- **Seen items**: an item seen in game but not looted no longer says "Not found in game yet".

## 1.2.1

### Bug Fixes
- **Sharing**: sharing finds with other players has been fixed.
- **Chat channels**: General is back on /1.
- **Changed items**: 13 items said "Changed by a hotfix" when they are changed from Classic.
- **Test items**: level-1 test copies of Molten Core gear and potions, and Blizzard's placeholder items, are gone from the list.

### Improvements
- **Dungeon finds**: items found inside a dungeon now show the dungeon's entrance on the map.
- **Item data**: updated to the current Forever build, with one newly found item and eleven items that are now out.
- **Drop locations**: more places items drop or are sold, and fresher mob locations on the maps.

## 1.2.0

### New Features
- **Item tooltips**: hover an item to see whether it is new or changed in Forever, and how to get it (Settings > Items > Tooltips).
- **Alt+click**: Alt+click an item in your bags or a chat link to open it in the Item Database.
- **Guild and group sharing**: finds also reach your guild and group.

### Bug Fixes
- **Mounts**: mounts no longer show as changed from Classic.

## 1.1.0

### New Features
- **What changed**: a button in the item box shows what changed from Classic.
- **Map counts**: see how many items each zone and dungeon holds (Settings > Places on the Map).
- **Zone filter**: show one zone's items in the Item Database.
- **Found bar**: see how many new items have been found so far.
- **Quest items**: a new Quest category.

### Improvements
- **Truer changes**: changed items now match what actually differs from Classic.
- **Map tooltips**: hover a marked item on the map for its full tooltip.

## 1.0.4

### Bug Fixes
- **Map pins in every zone**: favorites and marked items now show on the World Map in zones you have explored, instead of being hidden under the map.

## 1.0.3

### Improvements
- **Question marks last**: items with no picture (shown as a question mark) now sit at the bottom of the Item Database, so the list opens on items you can recognize.

### Bug Fixes
- **Map marks stay**: an item you mark with Mark on Map stays on your maps after a reload or a new session, until you take the mark off.

## 1.0.2

### Improvements
- **Troublemakers can be shut out**: players who abuse the sharing channel can be banned, and everyone's addon then ignores their finds and requests.
- **Fewer wrong mutes**: players who reload a lot, or run a newer version of the addon, are no longer muted by others.

## 1.0.1

### New Features
- **New finds count**: the Live Feed tab shows how many finds from other players came in since you last looked, and the minimap button's tooltip says it too.

### Improvements
- **Smooth logins**: when many finds arrive at once after you log in, the open window takes them in with a light update every couple of seconds instead of redrawing for each one.
- **Lighter map pins**: favorites on the World Map, continent maps and minimap use far less CPU when zooming, moving and loading in after login.
- **Sharing stays up**: if someone takes over or locks the sharing channel, the addon moves everyone to a backup channel on its own.
- **Only real names**: creature, zone and finder names on shared finds come only from the game and the addon's own data, so nobody can put their own words on a find.
- **Lighter in raids**: when a whole raid sees the same new loot, a few players' addons share it instead of everyone's.

### Bug Fixes
- **Every find gets shared**: items you find while no one else with the addon is online now reach other players once they come online, instead of staying only in your copy.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
